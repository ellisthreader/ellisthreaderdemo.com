Also fixed along the way

  • TodayThe Chelmsford page has no address, phone number or map.

    RedesignAddress, map, directions, staffed hours and a contact form on every gym page.

  • TodaySays 24/7 but only lists “Staff hours”, with no explanation.

    RedesignLive “Open now, staffed until 9pm” status and a weekly hours chart.

  • TodayAdvertises week and month passes and student plans the checkout doesn’t sell.

    RedesignPrices come straight from what each gym’s checkout actually sells.

  • TodayThe £35 off-peak plan sold at every gym isn’t shown anywhere.

    RedesignOff-peak has its own card, colour and hours.

  • Today“NO CONTACTS” buttons on the homepage that go nowhere.

    RedesignEvery button says what it does and works.

  • TodayThe Birmingham page says “Trident Weston gives you everything you need”.

    RedesignEach gym page is written for that gym.

  • TodayThe FAQ says 8 locations; the site lists 11. The footer says © 2019.

    RedesignGym counts, years and details come from one source, so they never drift.
