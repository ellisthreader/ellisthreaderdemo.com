Questions

All questions

Where is Trident Coventry?

50 Bishop Street, Coventry, CV1 1HW. There is free parking on site.

When is the team on site?

Members can train 24/7. Staff are on site Monday to Friday, 8am–9pm; Saturday and Sunday, 8am–4pm.

Can I buy a day pass at Coventry?

Yes. Day passes are £7 at reception during staffed hours.

What equipment is there?

Dumbbells up to 70kg, 3 squat racks with lifting platforms, boxing bags, functional area and skiErg, plus plate-loaded and pin-loaded machines and a full cardio area.

Is there a sauna?

The recovery area has: two saunas. It is included with every membership.

Message Coventry

Email coventry@tridentgym.uk. Or send a message and the team will reply by email.
